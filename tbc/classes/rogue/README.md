## Rotation and talent model

### Player rotation

- Opener: The source includes stealth openers such as Pick Pocket with Cheap Shot and control used to bypass dungeon targets.
- Sustained priority: Sinister Strike generation, Riposte procs, finishers, interrupts, and cooldown use are described at a high level.
- Cooldowns / emergencies: Blade Flurry, Adrenaline Rush, Vanish, Evasion, Sprint, Cloak of Shadows, and bandages appear in the source.

### Macro opportunities

- Derive attack-start, stop-attack, mouseover interrupt, stealth safety, cooldown, poison, and focus macros from the Combat plan.
- Select leveling, farming, or PvP before promoting a small set from the source backlog.

### Deliberately not macroed

- Combo-point spending, energy timing, positioning, and escape decisions remain manual.
- Poison ranks and bag items remain unpromoted until the player's inventory and client names are supplied.
